    02 / Services

    What we do, and when we do it

    Weekly maintenance keeps a yard from getting away from you. The rest is timed to the season — do it in the right month and it works, do it in the wrong one and you pay twice.

    Apr–Oct · Weekly

    Lawn maintenance

    Same day every week, same crew. We cut at the right height for the season instead of scalping it in July, and everything gets blown off before we leave.

    • Mow, stripe and blow off
    • String trim and hard edge
    • Beds weeded on the same visit
    • Fertilizer and weed control rounds
    Mar–Apr / Oct–Dec

    Spring & fall cleanups

    Beds cut back, winter debris and leaves hauled off the property, gutters of the yard cleared out. The reset that makes everything else easier.

    Mar–Jun

    Mulch & bed edging

    Hand-cut trench edge, then double-shredded hardwood laid at depth. It holds moisture through the summer and keeps the grass out of your beds.

    Sep–Oct

    Aeration & overseeding

    Core aeration to open compacted clay, then seed and starter fertilizer while the ground is still warm. The one job that actually fixes a thin lawn.

    As needed

    Storm & debris cleanup

    Downed limbs, blown-out beds, washed gravel. Call after the storm and we’ll get the property back in shape and the debris off it.

    Year-round

    Hardscaping

    Base work is where a patio lives or dies, so we dig it, compact it and screed it properly before a single paver goes down. Winter is a good time to build — the ground is workable and the schedule is open.

    • Paver patios
    • Walkways and steps
    • Block retaining walls
    • Fire pits and sitting walls
    • Gravel drives
    • Crushed shell drives
