The program

We Tested Two Models. One Wasn’t Close.

Over the past year, we ran two training models side by side with our athletes at G1. The results made the decision for us — starting September 1st, we are no longer a pay-per-lesson based business.

Retired September 1st

Pay-Per-Lesson

The model most hitters have experienced — with us and everywhere else.

  • A one-off lesson once per week, every two weeks, or once a month
  • One hour of coaching, then a week on your own with no plan
  • Slower adjustments, less retention, smaller jumps on the field
  • Nothing included but an hour of your time in a cage
  • Nothing to do in between lessons but wait — no plan, no system
  • No refunds, no guarantees — the risk is all on you
The only way we train now

Full Stack Hitting Development

A monthly membership where you train with us every single day — not one hour per week.

  • In-person training at our facility
  • Remote coaching from Austin and the G1 staff every day
  • Every plan includes strength, conditioning & nutrition programming, updated each month
  • Full access to our complete video course library, covering every part of your plan
  • Faster development, quicker adjustments, better retention, and the biggest jumps in on-field success we’ve ever seen
  • 90‑day money‑back guarantee

The results from this model blew the pay-per-lesson results out of the water — so much better that we scrapped the other model completely. To be very transparent: we no longer offer pay-per-lesson. The monthly development program is the only way athletes train with us.

Who we are

A Higher Tier of Training

G1 was founded for the baseball/softball player that is looking for a higher tier of training for their hitting development. Hitting is the hardest athletic activity in all of sports, and we believe your training should both recognize and be reflective of that. Demand for our training is extremely high, and this program exists to prioritize the players that are serious about their hitting development and getting to the next level — which is why we only have the capacity to work with 30 athletes. We offer a comprehensive approach to your development, inside of a facility that has every resource a hitter could ever need to be successful, built on systems that are actionable, repeatable, and will keep you on a consistent path of improvement.

Common questions

Before You Start

Can I still book a single lesson?
No. To be very transparent, as of September 1st we no longer offer pay-per-lesson. We tested both models side by side for a year, and the development program produced results that weren’t close — so the monthly development program is now the only way athletes train with us.
How many spots are available?
We only have the capacity to work with 30 athletes. Current clients, past clients, and everyone on our waitlist get priority access before the program is released to the public, and we anticipate these spots will go very fast.
How often will I actually train?
Every day. You’ll train in-person with us (2:1) at our facility either once or twice per week, and get remote coaching from Austin and the G1 staff every other day of the month — including weekly practice plans, drill sets with step-by-step video, and a clear roadmap for what to work on each day.
How does the 90-day money-back guarantee work?
Simply put: if you or your child are not happy with your swing after 90 days, we refund you in full. No questions asked. You either love your swing after 90 days, or you don’t pay.
Do you train softball players?
Yes — G1 was founded for the baseball/softball player that is looking for a higher tier of training for their hitting development. The program is built for both.
I filled out a form with you before. Do I need to apply again?
Yes — please fill out the new application form even if you have filled out a previous form with us. It’s how we’re assigning the 30 spots.

Absolutely. Fill out the application on your athlete’s behalf and we’ll follow up with you directly.
