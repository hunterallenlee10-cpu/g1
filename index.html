What we do

Everything a Hitter Needs, In One Place

Training Systems

We will use our proven training systems to help you transform your swing to catch more barrels, hit for more pop, and use less effort to do it. Our systems take the guess work out of it, and allow you to take actionable steps to be game ready when you step in the box.

In-House Training

Train in house with us at our indoor facility, where we have state of the art equipment, pitching machines, HitTrax, and all other training aids that a hitter would ever need to reach their potential.

Quantifiable Progress Tracking

We have multiple avenues to track your progress throughout your time with us. Our facility can offer you multiple camera angles of your swing, progress reports from our HitTrax, and other feedback loops. We also have our own proprietary reports and strategies that we use to evaluate and track your progress.

Remote Training

Can’t make it to our facility? Our remote platform allows us to help you reach your potential from anywhere in the US.

Who we are

A Higher Tier of Training

G1 was founded for the baseball/softball player that is looking for a higher tier of training for their hitting development. Hitting is the hardest athletic activity in all of sports, and we believe your training should both recognize and be reflective of that. We aim to make premium coaching, equipment, and training environments available to the select group of athletes that realize they need to make a change in order to reach their full potential. We offer a comprehensive approach to your development, inside of a facility that has every resource a hitter could ever need to be successful. We have spent years developing systems that are actionable, repeatable, and will keep you on a consistent path of improvement.

Common questions

Before You Start

Do I have to train in person, or do you offer remote training?
Both. You can train in house at our indoor facility, and if you can’t make it in, we have multiple remote options — our remote platform allows us to help you reach your potential from anywhere in the US.
Who is Membership Training for?
Our membership model allows high school hitters and older to hit with us, with a coach in our facility, up to 5 days per week. Younger players can train with us through 1-on-1 hitting training.
Do you train softball players?
Yes — G1 was founded for the baseball/softball player that is looking for a higher tier of training for their hitting development.
Can my whole team train with you?
Yes. Reach out and inquire about team training sessions in our facility or at your practice.
How fast will I hear back after I send a request?
One of our representatives will contact you within 48 business hours. You can also reach us directly at Info@g1-baseball.com or 301-801-4639.
I’m a parent filling this out for my athlete. Is that okay?
Absolutely. The training request form has a spot for a parent or guardian’s name — fill it out on your athlete’s behalf and we’ll follow up with you.
