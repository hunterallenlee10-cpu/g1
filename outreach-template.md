## Tips

- **Personalize the first line.** Reference the athlete by name, a recent game, or the last time you talked. The more it reads like a real message from you, the better the response rate.
- **Ask both questions separately if needed.** If someone says no for their own kid, the referral ask still works: "Totally understand — do you know anyone who might be a fit?"
- **Follow up once.** If you don't hear back in 3–4 days, one short bump ("Hey, any thoughts on this? Spots are filling") is enough.
- **Track it.** Keep a simple list: who you messaged, their response, and any referrals they gave, so nobody falls through the cracks.
